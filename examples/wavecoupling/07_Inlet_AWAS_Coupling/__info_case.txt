The Inlet_AWAS_Coupling is based on the work published by Lowe et al. (2022). 
The study employed the DualSPHysics model to investigate the hydrodynamics of plunging irregular waves on a coastal reef.
The model was validated against experimental results from Buckley et al. (2015).

Prior to this, the SWASH model had been validated against experimental data. The data from SWASH were then used to impose boundary conditions in DualSPHysics. 
To achieve this, an inlet buffer (i.e. open boundary) was employed.

The Matlab script 'lay2fix.m' converts SWASH output to create the necessary .csv files for DualSPHysics to run. 

Specifically, it extracts the surface elevation from SWASH at two locations: the inlet location (creating CaseBuckley_zurf.csv and CaseBuckley_inlet.csv)
and the location of the numerical wave gauge for the Active Wave Absorption System (AWAS) (creating CaseBuckley_awasIn.csv).

The batch file w01_CaseInlet_AWAS_Coupling_SWASH_win64.bat executes SWASH (v.5.01) to extract velocity and surface elevation data at the coupling location. 
It then runs a Matlab script to convert the data.  

The batch file w02_CaseInlet_AWAS_Coupling_win64_GPU.bat (or the CPU version) executes DualSPHysics and the post-processing tools. 

References:
Lowe, R. [et al.]. Smoothed Particle Hydrodynamics simulations of reef surf zone processes driven by plunging irregular waves. "Ocean modelling", 2022. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ocemod.2022.101945
Buckley, M. [et al.]. Dynamics of wave setup over a steeply sloping fringing reef. "Journal of Physical Oceanography", 2015. https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-D-15-0067.1
